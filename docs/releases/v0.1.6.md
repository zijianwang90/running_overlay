Running Overlay Studio **v0.1.6** (build **9**) is a stable release for macOS 15+.

This release adds real project files with autosave and crash recovery, reworks
the window toolbar, and makes timeline, playback, and overlay editing faster.
It also includes the v0.1.5 changes, because v0.1.5 was never published as a
GitHub Release.

## Highlights

### Projects

- **Save and open projects** - save your work as a single `.roproj` file with
  New Project, Open Project, Open Recent, Save, and Save As. Double-click a
  project in Finder to open it.
- **Your videos stay connected** - reopened projects find moved or renamed
  videos. If a file is missing, choose a folder to relink it by name.
- **Autosave and crash recovery** - unsaved work is copied every 30 seconds and
  when you switch apps, and you are offered recovery if the app quits
  unexpectedly.
- **Unsaved-changes prompts** - closing the window, quitting, or opening another
  project asks before anything is lost, and the window stays open if you cancel.

### Editor

- **New toolbar** - the project name and Edited state sit at the top center,
  with the pool switch on the left and Export on the right.
- **Collapsible side panels** - hide the Pools panel (Control-Command-S) or
  Inspector (Option-Command-I). Panel sizes are remembered between launches, and
  dragging them no longer jitters.
- **Clearer feedback** - failed actions show a banner that stays until dismissed,
  and finished imports and exports show a brief confirmation.

### Overlays

- **Duplicate overlays** - press Command-D, or right-click an overlay for
  Duplicate, Hide, Lock, and Delete.
- **Pixel-precise nudging** - arrow keys move the selected overlay by one output
  pixel, or ten with Shift, in all four directions.
- **Free placement** - hold Command while dragging to turn snapping off.
- **Overlay switcher** - jump between overlays from the top of the Inspector.

### Timeline and Playback

- **Smooth zoom** - pinch and Command-scroll zoom continuously around the
  pointer.
- **Camera clip menu** - right-click a clip to apply its offset to the whole
  camera track, reset the offset, or delete the clip.
- **More navigation keys** - J slows playback, Home and End jump to the timeline
  ends, Shift-Left and Shift-Right step ten frames, and Up and Down jump between
  clip boundaries.

### Media Pool

- **Show in Finder** - right-click media to reveal its files.

### Also Included From v0.1.5

- **GPX activity import** - open timed GPX tracks alongside FIT files.
- **Add Media appends** - importing more videos keeps the existing Media Pool and
  timeline.
- **Single-window editor** - the editor uses one window, and clicking the
  timeline ruler during playback seeks immediately.
- **Faster exports** - default HEVC-with-alpha exports are about 1.5x faster, with
  files about 30% larger at the same bitrate and a small alpha-quality trade-off.
- **Clean transparency in DaVinci Resolve** - exported MOVs now carry BT.709
  color tags.
- **Built-in templates restored** - the downloadable app loads its built-in
  templates, symbols, and weather icons correctly again.
