# Project Log — November 2026

## 2026-11-02

### Weather Widget Card Layout and Location Names

- Fixed the Simple Card icon drifting to the bottom-left and spilling outside
  the card. The condition label now occupies a fixed-height row, the icon is
  centred in the remaining space and capped to it, and card content is clipped
  to the card shape before the background is drawn. The same frame and clip
  treatment now applies to the Compact Strip, Forecast Tile, and Dashboard Bar
  presets.
- Fixed the card border not enclosing the fill: preset views pin their content
  to the element rect before drawing the background, and the border uses
  `strokeBorder` so the stroke sits inside the card instead of straddling it.
- Weather widgets now draw their editor selection outline on the card edge
  rather than five points outside it, so the outline no longer reads as a
  detached white border around the card.
- API-backed weather now resolves readable place names such as
  `Peterborough, ON`: macOS `CLGeocoder` first, then a keyless OpenStreetMap
  Nominatim reverse lookup, then the raw coordinates. Subdivisions are
  shortened to ISO 3166-2 codes for Canadian provinces, US states, and
  Australian states. Successful lookups are cached in memory.
- Updated the weather overlay guide, `PRIVACY.md`, and the external-services
  attribution notes.
- Verification: `./scripts/check.sh` passed. Focused
  `WeatherFetcherTests` cover province/state abbreviation, country fallback,
  the Nominatim request URL, and decoding. Card layouts were rendered through
  `ImageRenderer` at normal and oversized condition fonts to confirm the icon
  stays inside the card.
