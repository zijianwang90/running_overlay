# Resolve Release Gate Checklist

Assign owners and record new evidence in [testing.md](testing.md) as checks are completed. Partial evidence does not close a gate; focused human review is required for timing, persistence, undo, codecs, performance, licensing, credentials, privacy and compatibility.

| Gate | Evidence required | Current evidence | Status (done / partial / not started) | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Resolution and frame-rate matrix | Host preview and Deliver at UHD and non-16:9, and 23.976, 24, 25, 29.97, 30, 50 and 59.94 fps; verify values, geometry and timing. | [testing.md](testing.md#visual-presets-elevation-profile-and-uhd--2026-11-01) records Lua-only UHD/non-16:9 checks; [testing.md](testing.md#live-host-findings) records a basic UHD frame and host timing cases excluding 23.976. | partial | Unassigned |
| Full-length composite and alpha-only Deliver | Complete full-length composite and isolated alpha-only exports, inspecting values, continuity and transparency. | [testing.md](testing.md#cut-overlay-deliver-validation--2026-10-31) records a short composite; [testing.md](testing.md#follow-up-host-validation--2026-09-28) records a 270-frame isolated alpha export. | partial | Unassigned |
| Color management | Compare viewer and Deliver output in YRGB and color-managed projects, documenting settings and color accuracy. | none | not started | Unassigned |
| Preview/export value and style parity | Compare viewer and Deliver values and styles across supported widgets, presets, boundaries and realistic compositions; document accepted differences. | [testing.md](testing.md#template-style-seeding--2026-11-01) records 1080p template renders; [testing.md](testing.md#numeric-text-controls--2026-10-31) records numeric export and notes live viewer interaction remains unverified. | partial | Unassigned |
| Undo contract | In host, verify one undo point for discrete edits and one transaction per continuous gesture, including redo and cut overlays. | [testing.md](testing.md#data-time-offset-and-simplified-panel--2026-10-17) records browser slider checks and identifies host undo/redo as required. | partial | Unassigned |
